# Operation: Silent Leak

A major node within the global technology supply chain recently suffered a sophisticated data extortion incident. In a deviation from traditional ransomware tactics, the threat group prioritised the exfiltration and publication of sensitive corporate data over the disruption of industrial control systems.

You have been activated as a Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) analyst to investigate this incident. The digital trail is fragmented across the open web. You must access our secure investigation platform to piece together the threat actor's identity, the attack methodology, and the full scope of the compromise.

## Mission Directives

Your objective is to answer the sequence of intelligence checkpoints on our secure terminal. 

**Important:** The final target location is strictly classified. You cannot simply guess or geolocate the facility using the briefing alone. Once you successfully validate all intelligence requirements on the platform, our internal system will automatically decrypt and reveal the highly-classified geographic coordinates required to complete the operation.

## Flag Format

`BSCTF{[East-West Coordinate]+[North-South Coordinate]}`

*(Note: The coordinates will be provided to you by the system upon completion. Use the exact numerical format provided by the terminal.)*
