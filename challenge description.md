# Operation: Silent Leak

A strategic manufacturing partner within the global technology supply chain has become the latest victim of a sophisticated cyber intrusion. Rather than disrupting operations, the adversary focused on extracting sensitive information and leveraging it for public exposure.

Your role is that of a Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) analyst assigned to reconstruct the incident using publicly available intelligence. The evidence has been intentionally fragmented across multiple sources. Correlate the available information, identify the threat actor, assess the impact, verify the victim's location, and reconstruct the complete intelligence picture.

Each correct finding unlocks another stage of the investigation. Once every intelligence requirement has been satisfied, determine the exact geographic coordinates of the affected facility and submit them in the required flag format.

## Flag Format

`BSCTF{[East-West Coordinate]+[North-South Coordinate]}`

*(Note: The coordinates should be numerical. Use the East/West position first, followed by a plus sign, and then the North/South position.)*
